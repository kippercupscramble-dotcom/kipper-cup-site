Why We Play

Golf for a Great Cause

In Memory of Kip

The Kipper Cup began in 2023 when Kip Green’s golf buddies decided to keep their tradition alive — and turn it into something bigger. Every round played honors his memory.

Funding Scholarships

Entry fees and sponsorships fund the bi-annual Kipper Scholarships, awarded to students and educators pursuing higher learning and certifications.

A Day on the Greens

Your $140 entry includes green fee, cart, range balls, and a catered buffet lunch — plus prizes, contests, and plenty of friendly competition.
